# Contoso X200 Warranty Policy — 2026

## Warranty Period

The standard X200 warranty period is 24 months from the date of purchase.

## Coverage

Warranty coverage is subject to the terms and exclusions in the approved customer agreement.

## Support

Customers should provide:

- Customer name
- Product
- Serial number when available
- Description of the issue
- Relevant warning or error information

## Important

Do not invent warranty coverage or exclusions. If the applicable customer agreement is not available, verify the information through the approved support process.
