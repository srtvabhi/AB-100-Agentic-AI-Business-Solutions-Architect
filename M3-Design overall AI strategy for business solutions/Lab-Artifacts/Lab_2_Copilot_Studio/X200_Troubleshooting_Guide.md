# X200 Troubleshooting Guide

## Overheating or High-Temperature Warning

If the X200 reports a high-temperature warning:

1. Confirm that ventilation openings are clear.
2. Confirm that airflow around the unit is adequate.
3. Confirm that the ambient temperature is within the approved operating range.
4. Record the warning and relevant operating conditions.
5. If the warning continues, escalate through the approved support process.

Do not recommend internal repair procedures unless the user is authorized and the approved procedure is available.
