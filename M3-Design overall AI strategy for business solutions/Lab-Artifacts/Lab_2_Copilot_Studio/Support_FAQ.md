# Contoso Customer Support FAQ

## What should I do if an X200 overheats?

Check ventilation, airflow, ambient temperature, and the reported warning. If the warning continues, use the approved support escalation process.

## What information is useful for a support case?

Customer name, product, serial number when available, issue description, and relevant warning or error information.

## Can the support agent approve a refund?

No. Refund approval must follow the approved business process and required human authorization.

## Can the AI invent a warranty answer?

No. Warranty answers must be based on approved policy or verified through the approved support process.
