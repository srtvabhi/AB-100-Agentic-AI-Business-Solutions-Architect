# Lab 2 Artifact — Microsoft 365 Sales and Service Knowledge

## Customer Communication Rules

- Customer-facing drafts must be reviewed by the seller or service representative before sending.
- Do not expose internal-only notes to customers.
- Use approved product and troubleshooting information.
- If required information is unavailable, state that the information could not be verified.

## Sales Guidance

For an opportunity summary, include:

- Account
- Opportunity
- Recent activity
- Open customer request
- Next recommended action

## Service Guidance

For a case summary, include:

- Case number
- Customer
- Issue
- Relevant history
- Applicable knowledge
- Recommended next step
- Escalation requirement, if applicable

## Fallback

If no approved knowledge source supports an answer:

1. Do not invent an answer.
2. Tell the user that the information could not be verified.
3. Direct the user to the appropriate business owner or knowledge source.
