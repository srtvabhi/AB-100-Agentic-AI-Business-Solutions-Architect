# AB-100: Architect AI Solutions for Business Productivity

## High-Level Agenda

| # | Module | What You Should Cover |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Introduction to agentic AI business solution architecture** | Introduce **generative AI, AI agents, and agentic AI**. Explain how agents are changing business applications and why organizations need an AI architecture rather than simply adding a chatbot. Establish the role of an AI solution architect. |
| **2** | **Analyze requirements for AI-powered business solutions** | Start from the **business problem**. Identify where AI/agents can create value, understand business processes, identify users and requirements, and assess whether the organization's data is suitable for AI grounding. |
| **3** | **Design an overall AI strategy for business solutions** | Move from individual AI use cases to an **enterprise AI strategy**. Decide how Microsoft 365 Copilot, Copilot Studio, Dynamics 365, Power Platform, Microsoft Foundry, agents, models, and data should fit together. Also consider single-agent vs. multi-agent approaches. |
| **4** | **Evaluate costs and benefits of AI-powered business solutions** | Determine whether an AI solution makes **business and financial sense**. Cover ROI, TCO, licensing/consumption considerations, build-vs-buy-vs-extend decisions, and choosing appropriate AI/model capabilities based on business requirements and cost. |
| **5** | **Design AI agents for business solutions** | Design the actual **agent architecture**. Understand agent capabilities, instructions, knowledge, grounding, tools/actions, orchestration, autonomy, and how agents interact with business applications and data. |
| **6** | **Design extensibility of AI solutions** | Learn how to go beyond out-of-the-box capabilities. Cover **connectors, APIs, MCP, custom agents, custom models, Microsoft Foundry, Copilot Studio extensibility**, and other mechanisms for connecting AI to business systems. |
| **7** | **Orchestrate configuration of prebuilt agents and apps** | Before building something custom, understand what **Microsoft already provides**. Learn how to identify, configure, customize, and combine prebuilt Copilot/agent capabilities across Microsoft 365, Dynamics 365, and Power Platform. |
| **8** | **Monitor, analyze, and tune AI agents** | Understand what happens **after deployment**. Monitor agent performance, user feedback, telemetry, quality, and business outcomes, then tune the agent, prompts, knowledge, or configuration to improve results. |
| **9** | **Manage testing of AI-powered business solutions** | Learn how to **test AI differently from traditional software**. Define test cases, evaluate responses and agent behavior, validate grounding and quality, and test end-to-end business scenarios before production. |
| **10** | **Design an ALM process for AI-powered business solutions** | Establish how AI solutions move safely from **development → test → UAT → production**. Cover environments, solution management, deployment, versioning, models, agents, connectors, and ongoing changes. |
| **11** | **Design responsible, secure, and governed AI solutions** | Bring everything together with **security, governance, compliance, privacy, risk management, responsible AI, identity/access, data protection, and organizational policies**. The goal is to make AI enterprise-ready, not just technically functional. |

## AB-100 in One Sentence

> **AB-100 takes you from business requirement → AI strategy → agent architecture → implementation → testing → deployment → operations → governance.**

The goal is to develop the mindset of an **AI Solution Architect**, rather than simply learning how to configure Microsoft Copilot.
