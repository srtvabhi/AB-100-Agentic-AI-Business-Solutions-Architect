# X200 Warranty Policy

## Standard Warranty
The X200 controller includes a 24-month limited warranty from the shipment date.

## Covered
- Manufacturing defects
- Component failure under normal operating conditions

## Not Covered
- Physical damage
- Incorrect installation
- Unauthorized modification
- Operation outside specified conditions

## Warranty Request
A warranty request must include the product serial number, purchase information, and a description of the failure.
