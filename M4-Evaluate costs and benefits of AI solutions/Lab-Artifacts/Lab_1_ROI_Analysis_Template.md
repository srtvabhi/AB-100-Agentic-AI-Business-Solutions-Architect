# ROI Analysis Template

## Business Outcome
Reduce customer-service case handling time.

## Baseline
- Monthly cases: 120,000
- Current handling time: 24 minutes/case

## Target
- Target handling time: 17 minutes/case

## Benefit Drivers
- Minutes saved per case
- Monthly hours saved
- Annual productivity value
- Customer-service capacity
- Potential customer experience improvement

## TCO Categories
- Implementation
- Platform/model usage
- Operations
- Training and change management
- Retirement/decommissioning

## Measures
- ROI
- Payback period
- NPV
- Cost-benefit ratio

## Validation
Replace illustrative assumptions with organization-approved financial and operational values before making a business decision.
