# Financial Document AI — Decision Scenario

Fabrikam Financial Services wants an AI capability that can:

1. Extract information from financial documents.
2. Summarize documents.
3. Use approved enterprise knowledge.
4. Integrate with existing business applications.
5. Respect identity, security, and regional requirements.

Evaluate three approaches:

- Build a custom AI solution.
- Buy an existing commercial capability.
- Extend an existing Microsoft capability.

Use the decision matrix and score each criterion from 1 to 5.

Suggested interpretation:

1 = poor fit
2 = weak fit
3 = acceptable
4 = strong fit
5 = very strong fit

Calculate weighted scores and document the reasons for the scores.

Do not treat the example weights as universal. Adjust them when the business requirements justify different priorities.
